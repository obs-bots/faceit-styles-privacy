Privacy Policy.

Transparency is important to us. Learn how FACEIT Styles handles information while helping you personalize your FACEIT browsing experience.

Last updated: October 21, 2026
Designed with privacy in mind

FACEIT Styles is designed to store customization settings locally and does not collect personal information, provided the installed version operates as described in this policy.

01 Introduction

FACEIT Styles is a browser extension developed by Coffee_MayZz. It allows users to customize the appearance of the FACEIT website, including backgrounds, visual effects, and interface settings.

02 Data Collection

FACEIT Styles does not collect or transmit personal information to external servers. It is not designed to collect personal details, authentication credentials, financial information, browsing history, or analytics data.

03 Local Storage

The extension may use Chrome storage to save customization preferences and settings for future sessions. This information is used to provide the extension's features.

04 Website Access

FACEIT Styles accesses the FACEIT website as needed to apply user-selected backgrounds, visual effects, and interface customizations. This access is intended to support the extension's core functionality.

05 Third-Party Services

FACEIT Styles does not use external analytics services if none are integrated into the extension. FACEIT is an independent third-party platform governed by its own privacy policy and terms of service.

06 Data Sharing

The extension does not sell or share collected personal information with third parties. It is designed to work without collecting personal information.

07 Data Security

Customization settings are stored using browser storage capabilities. Users can remove locally stored extension data through their browser settings or by uninstalling the extension.

08 Policy Updates

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or privacy practices change. The latest version will be available at this page.
